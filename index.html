What Is Salem?

Salem is a supportive community for people experiencing suicidal thoughts. It offers a space to share experiences, connect with others, and learn about appropriate professional support without being judged.

Frequently Asked Questions

Understand what Salem offers before deciding whether to join.

Who is Salem for?

Salem is intended for people experiencing suicidal thoughts who are looking for understanding, peer connection, and information about professional support.

Is Salem a substitute for therapy?

No. Community participation does not replace assessment, treatment, or other care from a qualified mental health professional.

Is my information confidential?

Before registering, review Salem's privacy policy to understand what information is collected, who can access it, and the limits of confidentiality within an online community.

What if I need urgent help?

Salem is not an emergency service, and registration forms or community messages may not receive immediate attention. If you may act on suicidal thoughts or cannot keep yourself safe, seek immediate help from local emergency services numbers provided below or go to the nearest emergency department.
